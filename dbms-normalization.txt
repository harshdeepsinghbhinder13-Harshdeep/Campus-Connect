DBMS — Normalization Guide
Semester 5 | Demonstration document, not official course material

Normalization reduces avoidable redundancy and update anomalies.

First normal form (1NF)
Every attribute contains an atomic value, with no repeating groups.
Instead of storing several course codes in one cell, store separate rows.

Second normal form (2NF)
The relation is in 1NF and every non-prime attribute is fully dependent
on each candidate key. This matters when a candidate key is composite.

Third normal form (3NF)
For each non-trivial functional dependency X -> A, either X is a
superkey or A is a prime attribute.

Example
Enrollment(StudentID, CourseID, StudentName, CourseName, Grade)
Separate student details into Student(StudentID, StudentName), course
details into Course(CourseID, CourseName), and retain
Enrollment(StudentID, CourseID, Grade).

Practice
Identify candidate keys, functional dependencies, and possible update
anomalies before proposing a lossless decomposition.
